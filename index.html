BEFORE YOU BUY

The questions worth asking first.

The ones a careful buyer asks, answered where you can read them before paying rather than after.

What am I actually receiving?
A zip file. Inside it is a folder of markdown files: a company brain, twelve plays, eight agent definitions, thirteen skills, and the guides. You open that folder in the Claude desktop app and it becomes a working system. There is no app to log into and no account on our side. The files sit on your machine and stay there.
Can I use it in Claude on the web, or in Cowork, or in Gemini?
The web version of Claude will not work, because it cannot read a folder on your computer. StoryOS is built and tested for the Claude desktop app, using its Code tab, on a paid plan. Other tools that can read a local folder may well run it, and we have not tested them, so we will not claim they do. Gemini is not supported.
Why not just do this in a chat window for free?
You can get one good battlecard from a chat window in ninety seconds. What you cannot get is the second list. A chat window will happily repeat your "40% faster onboarding" back to you as fact, because you said it. StoryOS treats your claims the way it treats theirs, and tells you which of yours carry no date and no source. That is the difference, and it is the whole product.
Can I add my own agents and capabilities?
Yes, and that is a real feature rather than a promise. Alfred, the guide inside the kit, builds new plays, new skills and whole new specialists on request. You describe the situation you keep hitting, he proposes the play, shows you the exact change, and writes nothing until you say yes. He never touches your brain or your finished work while extending the system.
What does it cost me beyond the $79?
A paid Claude plan, from $20 a month, which is not ours and which you may already have. Nothing else. Year one is about $319 if you are starting from zero, and $79 if you already pay for Claude. We would rather you see that number here than discover it at checkout.
How much work is it before it sounds like my company?
About 20 minutes to install. Loading your brain is the part that is yours, and how long it takes depends on what you already have written down. You can start with a link to your site, two things that sound like you and one deal you remember well, and sharpen it every time you run a play. The five day guide inside walks the thorough version.
What is your refund policy?
There are no refunds. It is a file, and once it is downloaded it cannot be returned. That is why the requirements and the setup cost are on this page rather than behind the checkout, and why a full unedited output is published for you to judge before you pay. Read that first.
Where does my data go?
Your brain, your outputs and your notes are files on your machine. We never see them and there is no server of ours involved. What you type travels to Anthropic the same way everything else in your Claude session does, under your own Claude plan and its terms. If your organisation does not permit outbound lookups, one setting turns research off and the system works from your files alone.
I am buying for a team, not for me.
The licence is one seat per person, and there is a team tier built for exactly this: one canonical company brain that every seat reads from, so eight people write from the same truth instead of eight versions of it. Team pricing →
Does it break when Anthropic changes the interface?
The kit is markdown files, so there is nothing to break in it. What can change is the click path inside Claude for opening a folder. When that moves, the install guide is updated and the update reaches the email you bought with, at no cost, because you own the kit rather than rent it.
Who built this, and who supports it?
Anat Weissman, one person, and support is a real reply from her at storyos@connecthedots.co. There is no support desk and no ticket queue. That is a genuine limit and you should price it in.
